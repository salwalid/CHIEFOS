Three pillars. Zero compromise.

Most AI tools ask you to trust their cloud. CHIEFOS asks you to trust your own.

🔌

Bring your own AI

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, local models — swap providers with a config change. No rewrites. No migrations. Your keys, your quota, your choice.

🔒

Self-hosted by design

Runs on your machine, your VPS, your air-gapped lab. Data never leaves your infrastructure. Not even metadata. Not even "anonymized" telemetry.

⚖️

Governed, not vibes

Built-in MaatSpec governance. Every agent action is classified by risk tier and enforced by structure — not by hoping the AI follows instructions.

How the layers stack

CHIEFOS sits between you and your models, adding governance, memory, and multi-model orchestration.
